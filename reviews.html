I write for Techsen

I'm a journalist and editor at Techsen, a Danish tech news and review site with the tagline “Tech for alle” (tech for everyone). I joined in February 2026 and work there part-time and remotely: reviewing gear, editing, and looking after PR and social media.

My articles are published under the name Mikkel Techsen and written in Danish, so the headlines below are too. I've added a short line in English to each one.
