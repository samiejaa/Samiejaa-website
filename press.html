Where it started

Mikkel is an 18-year-old musician and creator from Denmark (he/they). Music has been part of life for as long as he can remember, and in 2023 he started taking it seriously: making and refining a sound of his own.

That sound never stayed in one lane. Electronic, dance and scene-influenced production lived next to indie, pop and rock, and what a song feels like always mattered more than which genre it belongs to. The catalogue that is online today begins in 2025, and until November 2026 all of it lived under one name, Samiejaa.

In November 2026, a year after the first indie release, the music split into two artist names, so listeners can find the sound they're looking for: Samiejaa for electronic, EDM and dance, and Mikkel Hammershøy for indie.

Main project · releasing since 2025

Samiejaa

Electronic, EDM and dance. Energetic music made to move to.

Samiejaa is where it all started. It is home to the electronic, EDM and dance side of Mikkel's music, with pop and rap along the way: high-energy songs for when you want to move. It's the genre Mikkel grew up looking up to artists in, and today it's mostly made for the fun of it.

Diary of Desire is the debut album, released on 11 October 2026. It is available on all major streaming platforms, on vinyl and CD, and comes with a matching merch collection.

Secret Little Dancer, one of the songs on the album, was released ahead of it as an early taste. Around the album sits a catalogue of singles and collaborations going back to July 2025, all on the music page.

COME ON! is a major release for Samiejaa: a collaboration with wikuro, a much bigger artist with around 10,000 monthly listeners on Spotify, featuring Xaduma, who also produced the track and has around 100,000 monthly listeners himself.

Side project · own name since Nov 2026

Mikkel Hammershøy

Indie, rock and pop with something to say.

Mikkel Hammershøy is part of Mikkel's real name, and that is exactly why it was chosen: the music here is closer to the person making it. It's indie, rock and pop, darker and more serious than the Samiejaa material, with lyrics that go deeper and mean a lot more.

The first songs to move over from the Samiejaa catalogue are dead2u (with Myron Blueboy), Love Beyond Death, Goodbye (with Myron Blueboy, back when he was still called myroblue) and Yearn For You.

The first indie release, Yearn For You, came in November 2025. A year later, in November 2026, the indie side got a name of its own, so listeners (and the streaming algorithms) can find the sound they're looking for.

Mikkel Hammershøy is the official side project. Samiejaa remains the main act, but that doesn't make the indie side any less important.
