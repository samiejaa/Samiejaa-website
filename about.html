WHO IS SAMIEJAA?

A little something about me :3

Hi, I'm Mikkel (he/they), an 18-year-old musician and creator from Denmark. I've loved music for as long as I can remember, but in 2023 I started taking it seriously, and I've been making and refining my sound ever since. The music that's online today starts on 25 July 2025 with BORDERLINE, my oldest song that's still out.

It never really stayed in one genre. I care more about what a song feels like than what box it fits in, so electronic, dance and scene-inspired sounds have always lived next to indie, pop and rock in my music.

For a while all of it lived under one name. In November 2026, a year after my first indie release, I decided to split my music into two artist names, to make it easier for you (and for the algorithm) to find the music you actually like. So now I'm two artists.

Two names, one person

My main thing

Samiejaa

Electronic and EDM. Music that makes you want to move.

Samiejaa is where I started. It's all of my electronic, EDM and dance stuff: energetic songs that could make you want to dance. I looked up to a lot of artists in this genre when I began, and these days I mostly do it for fun.

I recently released my debut album, Diary of Desire, and I'm really proud of it. You can find it on all streaming platforms, and on vinyl and CD in the shop.

COME ON! is a really big one for me: a song with wikuro, who's a lot bigger than me with around 10k monthly listeners on Spotify, featuring Xaduma, who produced it and has around 100k.

My official side project

Mikkel Hammershøy

Indie, rock and pop. The more personal side.

Mikkel Hammershøy is part of my real name, and I picked it because the music here is closer to me as a person. It's indie, rock and pop, darker and more serious, and the lyrics are a lot deeper and mean more to me.

I'm moving my indie songs over to this name: dead2u, Love Beyond Death, Goodbye and Yearn For You, which was my very first indie song, back in November 2025.

Samiejaa stays my main thing, but that doesn't mean Mikkel Hammershøy is any less important.

Outside of music: Techsen

I'm a journalist and editor at Techsen, a Danish tech news and review site with the tagline "Tech for alle". Since February 2026 I've worked there part-time and remotely, writing and editing reviews and handling PR and social media.

I even built the app and website the Techsen team uses to keep track of its test products and who publishes what, and when. I also study communication, IT and design, and I make my own visuals and build my own websites, this one included.
